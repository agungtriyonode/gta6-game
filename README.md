An open-world crime game in a city of its own, built for make.fun's
**"Make GTA 6"** bounty. No Rockstar names, maps, characters or art —
Solmar Bay, its people and its story are all original.

## The game

You are Rico, new in Solmar Bay, running jobs for Mara, the fixer at the
garage in Downtown. Explore a sun-soaked coastal city **on foot and by car**:
traffic on the avenues, pedestrians on the sidewalks, cars to steal, a
wanted level with police cars and foot cops who chase you down, and a
**five-mission story** — steal wheels, run a package, lose the heat, settle
a score with the Vipers, and pull the Big Score at the Marina.

- **Open world** — a road grid with Downtown, Old Town, the Docks, the
  Marina and Sunset Beach, all explorable with no loading screens.
- **Cars** — walk up to any traffic car and take it (E). Drive with W/S
  and steer with A/D. Run over the wrong person and the law notices.
- **Wanted level** — 1 to 5 stars. Crimes raise it; laying low and staying
  out of sight lets it cool off. Get caught with no health and you are
  busted: a $150 fine and a trip to Solmar General.
- **Combat** — fists (Space or click). Knock out rivals, or knock a
  pedestrian down and take the cash they drop.
- **Missions** — five jobs with dialogue from Mara, a yellow ring marking
  the next objective, and a payday at the end of each one.
- **HUD + minimap** — health, cash, district and clock, wanted stars, the
  current objective, and a live minimap with the mission marker, traffic
  and police.
- **Start + save** — the story saves to localStorage (money, mission,
  position) and continues where you left it. Best haul is remembered too.
- **Sound** — engine hum, police sirens and mission jingles, all
  synthesised in the browser. M mutes.

## Controls

| Key | Action |
| --- | --- |
| WASD / arrows | Walk (or drive, when in a car) |
| Shift | Run |
| E | Steal a car / get out |
| Space / click | Punch |
| P / Esc | Pause (saves) |
| M | Mute |
| H | Help (title screen) |
| N | New story (title screen) |

## Play it

Open `game/index.html` in a desktop browser — no build step, no network,
no install. Or serve it locally:

```sh
node serve.ts
# game/ is on http://localhost:8080
```
